    Every Screen. Infinite LeBron.

    Three screens, drawn up like a playbook. Here's how the app is organized.

    Home Court

    Your landing page. The banner showcases LeBron in all his glory, with a "Let The King Decide" shuffle button front and center. Below that, you'll find:

    • LeBron's Pick of the Day - A daily featured song with tap-to-play
    • Recent Highlights - Your recently played tracks in a scrollable row
    • MVP Selections - Your most played hits with top-3 badges
    • Playbooks - Quick access to your custom playlists
    • The Full Roster - Every song in the collection
    • Taco Tuesday Banner - Special banner every Tuesday!

    Now Playing

    A gorgeous full-screen player with dynamic gradient backgrounds that shift with each song. Designed for the ultimate listening experience:

    • Large Album Art - Beautiful cover art with shadow effects
    • Scrub Bar - Drag to seek with a thumb that appears on touch
    • Full Controls - Shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, repeat modes
    • Action Row - All-Star (favorite), Queue, Playbook (add to playlist), Mix (random queue)
    • On The Bench - See what's coming up next in the queue
    • Empty State - A special LeBron quote when nothing is playing

    The Vault

    Your personal music library, organized with basketball flair. Everything is stored securely in The Vault:

    • Playbooks Tab - All your custom playlists with song counts
    • All-Stars Tab - Quick access to all your favorited songs
    • System Playlists - Auto-generated collections like Recently Played, Top Hits, and Favorites
    • Custom Covers - Upload your own playlist cover images
    • Sort Options - By title, artist, play count, or most recent
    • Draft a Playbook - Create new playlists with the blue action button
